# Privacy-Policy

AlmaSport Privacy Policy
App: AlmaSport Last updated: November 24, 2026 Contact: 230682@almau.edu.kz
1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains what data the AlmaSport app (the “App”) collects, how it is used, and how you can control it. The App is operated by Ashirbek Amir (“we”, “us”). By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. We process personal data in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
2. Data We Collect
When you create an account and use the App, we collect:
• Name that you provide when you sign up;
• Email address used to sign in and to contact you;
• Phone number, only if you sign in to the App with your phone number;
• Device token, a technical identifier needed to deliver push notifications.
We do not collect your location, photos, contacts, or other data from your device.
3. How We Use Your Data
• to create and maintain your account and let you sign in;
• to provide the features of the App;
• to send push notifications (for example, about bookings, events, and news);
• to process payments and confirm that a payment was completed;
• to respond to your support requests;
• to improve the App.
We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
4. Payments
Payments in the App are processed by a third-party payment provider Whop. Your card details are entered on the payment provider’s side. We do not receive or store your card number or other payment credentials. We only receive confirmation that a payment was made (status and amount). The payment provider processes your data under its own privacy policy.
5. Push Notifications
Push notifications are sent only with your permission. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
6. Sharing Your Data
We share data only with the services the App needs in order to work:
• the payment provider, to process payments;
• the push notification service, to deliver notifications to your device.
We may also disclose data if required by law or by a request from an authorized government authority.
7. Data Retention and Security
We keep your data for as long as you have an account in the App. After you delete your account, your data is deleted, except for data we are legally required to retain (for example, payment records). We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.
8. Your Rights and Account Deletion
You have the right to:
• request access to your data and ask us to correct it;
• delete your account and all related data directly in the App (Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing us at 230682@almau.edu.kz;
• withdraw your consent to data processing.
We usually respond to requests within 30 days.
9. Children
The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you learn that a child has given us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.
10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this link, and the date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. We will notify you of material changes in the App.
11. Contact Us
If you have any questions about your data or privacy, email us at 230682@almau.edu.kz.
